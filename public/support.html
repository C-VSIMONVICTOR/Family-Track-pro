Help and support

Updated 18 October 2026

Getting started

  1. Create your own account and confirm your email.
  2. Create a family group or accept an invitation.
  3. Use Share my location when you want to appear on the family map.
  4. Keep the app open to send updates and see chat and alerts.

Account or app problem?

Sign in and open a private support request. Choose Help and support in Settings. Include the feature and error message, but never your password or precise location.

If you cannot sign in, try Forgot password. Email delivery setup is still being completed; this early release should not yet be relied on for a paid service.

Abuse or safety

Use Report next to the chat message. You can also hide a person’s messages. Family owners can remove a member. FamilyTrack does not provide emergency response.
