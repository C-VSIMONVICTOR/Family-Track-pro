Terms of use

Updated 18 October 2026

Use FamilyTrack Pro only with the knowledge and consent of the people involved. This release is intended for adults. Do not use it for covert monitoring, harassment, threats, unlawful activity or sharing content you do not have permission to share.

Service limitations

Location sharing works while the app is open and permissions and network access are available. Readings can be inaccurate or delayed. Alerts and messages require recipients to open the app; push notifications and background tracking are not available. FamilyTrack is not an emergency response service. Contact local emergency services directly for urgent help.

Your family group

Keep your password and invitations private. Family owners control group membership and can remove messages. Report abusive messages using the Report button. Service operators may remove reported content after review.

Account control

You can stop sharing, clear your location history, leave a family or delete your account. Deleting a family owner’s account also deletes their family groups. Availability is not guaranteed during this early release.
