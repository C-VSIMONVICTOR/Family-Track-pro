Privacy

Updated 18 October 2026

FamilyTrack Pro stores your email, account name, family memberships, chat messages, saved places and family activity. If you explicitly start sharing, it stores your coordinates, accuracy, update time and available battery level.

Your choices

Location sharing starts off. Only current members of your private family can see your latest shared position. Stop sharing in Home or Settings to remove the latest position. Your location history is visible only to you. History older than 24 hours is hidden, and old history is pruned when you next publish a position. There is currently no scheduled database purge.

Messages and reports

Current family members can read chat. You can delete your messages, report inappropriate content and hide another member’s messages. Family owners can remove messages and members. A service operator can review reported messages and support tickets. Chat is not end-to-end encrypted.

Service providers

Supabase provides account and database services. OpenAI Sites hosts the website. OpenStreetMap supplies map tiles; map requests disclose your IP address and viewed map area to the tile provider. Device permissions are requested only when you choose a location feature. We do not sell location data or use advertising trackers in this app.

Retention and deletion

Account and family data remain until you or the family owner delete them. Chat reports are removed with their family or reporter account. Delete your account using the web instructions below. This removes your account and associated data from the active database; provider backups may remain subject to the provider’s backup lifecycle. Groups you own and their data are also deleted.
