Delete your FamilyTrack account

Updated 18 October 2026

You can delete your account on this website without installing the Android app.

  1. Sign in to FamilyTrack with the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings (More on a phone).
  3. Choose Delete my account and confirm the deletion.

This permanently removes your account, memberships, locations and messages from the active database. Every family group you own, including its other memberships, chat, places and activity, is also deleted. Other members’ accounts remain. Provider backups may persist according to the provider’s backup lifecycle.

If you forgot your password, use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. Do not send your password to anyone. For other issues, open Support.
